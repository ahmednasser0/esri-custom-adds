Esri HR Management

Attendance, deductions and overtime, worked out automatically every day

Odoo 19  ·  Human Resources

Esri HR Management turns raw attendance into the three figures payroll actually needs — hours worked, deduction and overtime — and keeps them correct as attendance, time off and public holidays change.

Every rule is configuration, not code: working hours, weekend days, core hours, rates and deduction tiers are all set from the interface, per working schedule or company-wide.

Runs by itself

A punch, an approved leave or a new public holiday recalculates that day on its own. Scheduled jobs cover everyone daily, so no record is ever missed.

Correct by timezone

Every hour is read on the employee's own wall clock, from their working schedule's timezone — daylight saving included, never a fixed offset.

Per-employee rules

Staff, Office Assistant and Office Boy each get their own overtime treatment, and each working schedule carries its own hours, core window and weekend days.

Final once posted

A posted deduction is frozen for good: its values cannot be edited and it can never return to draft, so a closed payroll period stays closed.

Three records per employee, per day

All three live under Attendances → Advanced Allocation, are computed and stored, and can be filtered, grouped and totalled like any Odoo list.

Record What it holds
Daily Report Last check-out, effective check-out, time past the cutoff, total and actual worked hours — attendance and approved time off combined.
Deduction Core check, daily check and the resulting deduction, with a monthly grace for the smallest shortfall and a workflow through to posted.
Overtime Overtime start time, hours split around sunset, and the total weighted overtime after rates are applied.

Deduction policy

The shortfall is the greater of the core check — time left uncovered inside the core window — and the daily check, the hours missing from a full day. Every rate below is configurable.

Shortfall Charged Note
None Nothing Full day completed
1 – 15 minutes Quarter day Free the first time each month
16 – 59 minutes Half day  
A full hour or more Whole day  
Full absence Day and a quarter Unauthorised absence

Weekends and public holidays never carry a deduction. The amount charged is counted against Company Day Hours, while whether a day is short at all is judged against the employee's own Daily Work Hours.

Overtime

No overtime without an approved request. Hours are earned against an approved Overtime Request time off, on every kind of day — Office Boy being the one rule that reads attendance directly instead.
Day How it is worked out
Weekday The approved hours, weighted by the before-sunset and after-sunset rates. Attendance decides only when the overtime starts — the moment the full day is complete.
First weekend day A flat rate on a full day's hours, for any approved time at all.
Second weekend day Tiered: under half a day rounds up to half, half a day or more rounds up to a full one.
Public holiday Treated exactly as the first weekend day. Holidays are read from the working schedule's own time off lines.

Configuration

Where What it controls
Working Schedule Timezone, daily work hours, first check-in, last check-out, the core window, sunset, and which two days are the weekend — so a team that rests on other days is a setting, not a change of code.
Esri Configurations Overtime rates and cutoff, weekend rates, maximum worked hours, company day hours, the deduction tiers and the absence rate, and the time off type that acts as the overtime request.
Employee The employee rule — Staff, Office Assistant or Office Boy — which selects how overtime is earned.

Before you start

  1. Set the timezone on every working schedule — every hour in the module is read through it.
  2. Choose the Overtime Request time off type in Esri Configurations.
  3. Give every employee an Employee Rule.
