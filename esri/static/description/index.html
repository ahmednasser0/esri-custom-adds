Esri HR Management

Attendance, deductions and overtime, worked out automatically every day

Esri HR Management turns raw attendance into the three figures payroll actually needs — hours worked, deduction and overtime — and keeps them correct as attendance, time off and public holidays change.

A record per employee, per day

Daily Report Worked hours for the day, attendance and approved time off combined.
Deduction Tiered against the hours missing from the day, with a monthly grace.
Overtime Earned against an approved request and weighted around sunset, with its own weekend and public holiday rules.

All three are full Odoo lists under Attendances → Advanced Allocation — filter, group and total them like any other report, or export them to payroll.

  • Runs by itself. A punch, an approved leave or a new public holiday recalculates that day on its own, and scheduled jobs cover everyone daily.
  • Correct by timezone. Every hour is read on the employee's own wall clock, daylight saving included.
  • Configured, not coded. Working hours, weekend days, core hours, rates and deduction tiers are all settings — per working schedule or company-wide.
  • Final once posted. A posted deduction is frozen, so a closed payroll period stays closed.
